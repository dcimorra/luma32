# Adapting a book

When the game retells an existing work, fidelity is the quality bar (`SKILL.md`, non-negotiable 5).

- **First, the analysis.** Before the bible, the analyst — a member of the team, asked by
  mail (`mail/analyst.author.md`), with the instructions of `.claude/agents/luma-analyst.md`
  — researches the work and writes `SOURCE.md` in the cartridge: every chapter in order, every named
  character, every subplot from its setup to its payoff, places, objects, who knows what
  and when, what a reader would never forgive missing, and the gaps it couldn't confirm
  (`⚠`). The bible is written from it, not from memory; its gaps go to the commissioner
  as questions, never filled with something plausible.
- **The bible, chapter by chapter.** One file, written before the beat list: for every
  chapter of the source, its scenes in order — where, who is there, what happens,
  every conversation (who speaks, what each one says and reveals, in summary), what it
  changes (relationships, secrets, injuries, objects, who knows what). Plus a sheet per
  character: look, voice, how they address each other, what they know at each point.
  Detailed enough that whoever writes a scene never has to remember the book.
- **The beat list maps to the bible.** Each beat cites the bible scene it plays; each
  bible scene is played by a beat or listed as cut. **Every subplot of `SOURCE.md` is
  played or listed as cut**, and so is every moment of its "never forgive missing" list:
  subplots are what a short adaptation drops without anyone deciding it — and the best
  ground for side quests and played stretches (`length.md`).
- **Cuts are written down.** If the format forces leaving something out or compressing
  it (three training days into one), it goes in a "Cuts" section of the bible with what
  was cut and why. Nothing disappears silently; nothing is added that the book doesn't
  have, beyond the background people and lines that make a place alive.
- **Dialogue: same substance, new words.** Each line carries what the book's line
  carries — the information, the intent, the emotion, the character's voice — written
  fresh. No sentences copied from the book.
- **The fidelity review.** At the end, an agent that didn't write the game compares
  each chapter of the game (its locale files and story code) against the bible and
  `SOURCE.md` (its subplots and its "never forgive missing" list) and lists
  what is missing, what is extra and what is changed (events, order, who says what, who
  knows what, a voice that's off). Fix until the list holds only the documented cuts.
